# Design reference — Rubik's Cube Lamp

**The product**: an 8 × 8 × 3 in textured glass slab (Candy Glass = faceted cube grid; Ocean Glass = ripple; Bubble Glass) standing upright on a solid sheesham base with a small NX plaque. Three faces are hand-painted red, green and amber; the user **turns the slab on its base** to bring a face forward. It is never lifted off the base as a cube, it is not a 3×3 puzzle cube, and the glass is one block.
