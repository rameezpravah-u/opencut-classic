# Which NixWoods product is this? (identification guide)

Read this before naming a product in any reel, caption, end card or register row. Built 11 Oct 2026 from the
live Shopify catalogue (39 active products) after a floor-lamp clip was wrongly called the NixLine. On 16 Oct 38 are
active (Duo Glow is a draft). What each page lets a reel claim is in `PRODUCT-FACTS.md`.
Contact sheets of every product's main photo: `system/refs/catalog/catalog1-4.jpg` (slug and price on each tile);
the photo URLs are in `system/refs/catalog/featured-images.tsv`. Slugs match nixwoods-os `40-Content/PRODUCT-SLUGS.tsv`.

## How to identify
1. Find the **tell** in the footage: base shape, frame or no frame, how the light runs, size against furniture.
2. Match it to one row below. Name a product only when its tell is visible.
3. If two rows still fit, ask the owner. Never guess from "it's a lit wooden line": a dozen products are.
4. Product-page photos are partly AI renders and some show a different variant. Owner footage outranks them.
5. Prices change. Re-check live Shopify (`search_products`) before putting a price on an end card.
6. Claim only what the product page says. COD and free delivery are confirmed for the NixLine floor lamp only.
