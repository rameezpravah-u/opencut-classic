# Design reference — Aurora Linear Wall Light

**The product**: a single bar of solid wood mounted flat to the wall, with the LED recessed into a channel along one long edge so the light washes the wall and never faces the room. 36, 40 or 48 inches. Rosewood (dark brown stain) or natural teak. Two light directions: down only, or up and down. From ₹1,999. It is a bar, not a box, not a sconce with a shade, and the wood is one continuous piece with visible grain and rounded end caps.

**Reference imagery**: `../hf/sh*` are the live PDP photographs, pulled from the Shopify CDN on 6 Sep 2026 — real product photography, not generated. `sh04-unlit-real.jpg` is the bar unlit on bare plaster and is the honest "before" for any transformation cut. `sh03-angled-wash.jpg` and `sh06-angled-channel.png` show the recessed channel and the wash it throws.

**Not used in cuts**: the size chart and the colour-options card carry baked-in text that would fight our own captions. The up/down comparison image is a two-panel graphic, not a scene.

**AI note**: `sh02-entryway.jpg` is a store-generated visual (its filename says so) and is listed under `assets.ai_generated` in `system/presets.json`. Any ad using it needs Meta's AI disclosure ticked.

**Framing**: the bar is horizontal and long; a centre 9:16 crop cuts its ends off and throws away the one thing that makes it a linear light. Every wide shot is therefore set to `fit: contain` in the presets — the whole bar over a blurred, dimmed copy of its own scene. Only the diagonal macros are cropped to fill.
