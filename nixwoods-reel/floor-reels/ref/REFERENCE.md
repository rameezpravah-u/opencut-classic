# NixLine — what the real product looks like

**Corrected 19 Oct 2026 (owner):** the 5 Aug photo `AC4I9815-real-nixline-upright.jpg` is **not the NixLine**. It
shows a lit rod on a square block, the tell of the corner floor lamp (nixwoods-os already files it as
`NW-CONTENT-FB-20261015-corner-floor-lamp-still-photo-v1`). Everything below the line was written from that photo and
is wrong. The owner pointed to the live page, nixwoods.com/products/nix-line-solid-wood-floor-lamp.

**The real NixLine** (owner's own clips `floor-reels/src/nx00`–`nx08`, and every photo on the live page):
- one straight bar of **honey-coloured solid teak**, 1.5 x 1.5 in, 27 in on the page, softly rounded ends;
- a **frosted white LED diffuser** down nearly its whole front face, glowing warm (3000K);
- **no separate block or base**: it **leans** in a corner, foot on the floor, top against the wall; the cord leaves
  from its bottom end;
- the page also says a "weighted Teakwood base keeps it firmly upright": no clip or photo shows a separate base, so
  claim neither a base nor a block.

Best full-length real view: `brand-reels/cctv-heist/refs/nixline-owner-clip-nx04.jpg` (nx04 at 1.2 s).
