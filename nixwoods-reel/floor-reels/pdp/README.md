**Six of the seven are AI-generated** — the filenames are `ChatGPT_Image_Sep_19_2026_*`.
Anything from this folder in a paid ad means Meta's AI disclosure has to be ticked.
`F1-floor-nixline-ad` uses none of them and needs no disclosure.

Worth noting for its own sake: the PDP a buyer lands on is mostly generated renders,
while the product in `src/` is the real thing. An ad cut from real footage that lands
on a page of renders is a mismatch a buyer can notice.
