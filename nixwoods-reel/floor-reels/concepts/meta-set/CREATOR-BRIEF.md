# Creator brief — NixLine solid teak floor lamp (Meta partnership ads)

**Why:** our closest rival floor lamp (Ikari "Boho Beam") has kept one creator video running for
207 days; three more of their creator cuts have run 73–77 days. NixWoods has never run a real
creator video for this product, and the brand docs name "UGC from real homes" as the biggest gap.
Run them as Meta **partnership ads** (creator's handle on the ad; Meta reports +4% CTR, +3% conversions).

## The product — show it exactly as it is
- One straight solid-teak bar, ~30 in, standing **upright on a small square teak block**. It does
  **not** lean in a corner. LED channel on the front face, warm white. Switch on the cord, plug-in,
  no assembly. ₹999 (confirm MRP), free delivery, COD, 3 years on the wood, 1 year on electricals.
- Say only what is true: solid Indian teak, no MDF, no veneer, handmade in Uttar Pradesh.
  No brightness numbers, no delivery-time promises, no "best/luxury/premium", no invented
  results.

## Three videos, 15–30 s, 9:16, shot on a phone, in the creator's own home, at night
1. **"The corner lamp everyone asks about."** Open on the dark, empty corner with a tubelight or
   overhead light on. Lights off, switch the NixLine on **on camera** in the first 3 seconds, so the
   room changes. Walk the camera in to the grain, then sit down in the corner.
2. **Unboxing.** The handmade wooden crate, wood shavings, lifting the bar out, a close-up of the
   teak, placing it on its block, plugging in, switching on. Let the sounds play: crate, paper,
   click.
3. **Styling.** The same lamp in three spots in ~20 s (reading chair, bedside, beside the TV unit),
   ending on the one they keep it in, and why, in their own words.

## Must-haves
- The switch-on happens on camera, in the dark, inside the first 3 seconds.
- The lamp is upright on its block in every shot.
- Their own words; no script read to camera. A short line on price ("under a thousand rupees")
  and COD is fine.
- Captions burned in (most people watch muted). Keep the top 14% and bottom 35% of the frame
  clear of text.
- Hindi, Hinglish or English, whichever is natural for them.

## Deliverables
- Raw 9:16 files (4K or 1080p, 30 fps), plus the creator's own edit.
- Usage rights for Meta paid ads, 90 days minimum, and partnership-ad access to their handle.
- A signed note that they bought or were sent the product, and permission to quote anything they
  say. That becomes NixWoods' first real, permission-cleared social proof.

## Who
Women 35–55, home and decor creators in Tier-1 metros (the account's best buyers: women 35–64,
iPhone, metros), 10k–100k followers, real homes rather than studio sets. Budget and fees: set by
Rameez.
