# Brand

Written 8 Oct 2026 with the `brand-intake` skill. Every answer comes from what Rameez has already set in
this repo or in the shop; nothing below is a guess.

## Name and what we do
NixWoods. Solid-wood lighting, handmade in Uttar Pradesh, India.

## Audience
Indian home-makers, 25–65, who live with a white tubelight and want one warm corner without an
electrician or a big spend.

## What we promote (with the real link)
NixLine, a 30-inch solid-teak floor lamp that stands upright on its own teak block. 2700–3000K LED, plug
in, no assembly. ₹999 (compare-at ₹1,599), cash on delivery, free delivery.
https://nixwoods.com/products/nix-line-solid-wood-floor-lamp

Paid ads and links go to nixwoods.com only, never the Shopdeck or .in store.

## The feeling
Warm, calm, honest. The light does the selling; the type stays quiet.

## Never
- Never show the NixLine leaning. It stands upright on its teak block.
- Never use: discover, elevate, luxury, premium, exquisite, journey, curated, unlock, stunning, delve,
  realm, luxurious, "premium quality".
- Never invent a review, a rating, a testimonial or a delivery time.
- Never publish a generated frame without the Meta / Instagram AI label.
- "Rubik's Cube" stays in all cube-lamp creative (D-017). Do not raise it.
