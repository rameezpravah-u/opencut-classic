# NixWoods "10 to 01": the Minimalist floor lamp counted down through its making

**The idea:** Vijay Lamps' countdown reel (instagram.com/reel/DeR6W_cygHa, 16.1 s) counts from 10 to 01 through the
making of a brass bell lamp, from log to lit lamp. The owner asked on 16 Oct for "something on a product with the
below style", using the newly reachable Drive library and accurate product facts.

**Product:** the Minimalist Wooden Floor Lamp, chosen because it is the one product whose whole making is on Drive,
from the log yard to the lit lamp.
- **Tell:** a lit rod on a round wooden base (`PRODUCT-ID.md`). The 5 Aug shoot file for the last shot is named
  "sleek cylindrical", but the lamp on screen has the Minimalist's tell.
- **Facts:** solid rosewood; 44 in ₹6,999 (was ₹9,599) and 60 in ₹9,999 (Shopify, 16 Oct); plug-in; dimmable
  (`PRODUCT-FACTS.md`). There is no remote claim, because the page doesn't make one.

**Timing:** the reference dips to black between steps at 1.467, 3.100, 4.500, 5.867, 7.433, 8.833, 10.400, 11.867,
13.367 and 14.367 s, measured frame by frame. Our dips sit on the same frames, so its audio fits through "Use
audio". The clip's own cut from the dim pair to the lit pair lands at 13.83 s, where the reference's lamp comes on.

**Look:**
- Numbers: Inter 600, cream, centred at about 30% height, as in the reference.
- One small word under each number.
- MOTION.md montage grade (62% own colour).
- The small NIXWOODS mark low in the frame.
- Blacks lifted by 0.02.

**Two cuts:**
- `…-in-app-audio.mp4`: silent. Post it and tap "Use audio" on the reference.
- `…-own-sound.mp4`: each shot's own workshop sound, levelled, under soft music1. It can run as an ad.

The reference's audio is never committed. A preview with it is kept in the session scratchpad only.

the private nixwoods-os `30-Creative/library/INDEX.tsv`.
