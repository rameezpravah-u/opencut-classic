**The idea:** @iranway_studio's linear pendant reel (instagram.com/reel/Deew9uBo446, 10.2 s). The owner asked on
16 Oct: "Do this for Scandinavian pendant."

**The reference, frame by frame:**

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0.0–3.3 s | A moody wide of the pendant over a dining table. The camera tilts down to the table. |
| 3.3–6.0 s | The room fades to dark while three detail panels come in, top to bottom: the bar's end and cord (3.5 s), a diagonal close-up of the lit underside (5.0 s), the bar from the side (6.0 s). |
| 6.0–10.2 s | The three panels hold, each drifting slowly. |

There is no text. Our panels come in on the same times, so its audio fits through "Use audio".

**Product:** the Scandinavian Rosewood Pendant.
- **Facts:** rosewood 4 ft ₹7,599 (was ₹10,699), teak 4 ft ₹8,399, 6 ft ₹17,599. Hardwired; dimmable with the included
  remote (`PRODUCT-FACTS.md`, 16 Oct).
- **How it was identified:** the footage is the 5 Aug shoot's three clips of this lamp. Their file names say "nordic
  … curved", but the lamp on screen is:
  - a straight bar with round ends, on two cables;
  - in the same dining room as this product's own photos (`rosewood-reels/hf/sh00`, `sh01`).

  The Nordic pendant is a curved arm.
