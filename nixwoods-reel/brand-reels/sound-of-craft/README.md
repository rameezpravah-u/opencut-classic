# NixWoods "The sound of craft": our workshop, heard

**Reference:** instagram.com/reel/DeZUtdbASYZ by @thenohea (22.3 s, a furniture maker in Udaipur). The owner sent it on
13 Oct: "Another good idea for you to execute?"

## The pattern
- **Footage:** 14 workshop shots of 0.9–2.6 s each (CNC, saw, sander, router, welding, hammering).
- **Sound:** every shot plays with its own machine sound.
- **Text:** "THE / SOUND OF / CRAFT" in small white bold sans, staggered across the frame (THE upper right, SOUND OF
  left, CRAFT lower right), on screen throughout.
- **End:** the logo, 20.3–22.3 s.

## This version (`sound_of_craft.py`)
- **Timing:** the reference's 14 cut lengths, then a 2 s Dusk end card (logo in cream, nixwoods.com in amber).
- **Footage:** our real workshop clips, each with **the sound it was recorded with**:
  1. the CNC router cutting a channel;
  2. strips cut, in the sawdust;
  3. the router again;
  4. the teak planks;
  5. routed bars;
  6. the router, deeper in the cut;
  7. black-finish bars;
  8. bars bundled, then lit;
  9. a bar lit, standing (straightened);
  10. a lit bar on the floor;
  11. a bar switched on (its click);
  12. a lit bar at the bench (straightened);
  13. a long bar lit on test;
  14. the Aurora coming on a wall, given the test bar's switch click, since that clip has no sound.
- **Audio levels:** loud machine shots are levelled to −20 dB RMS. Quiet room tone is lifted at most 12 dB, so the
  CNC and the saw lead and the handling shots stay hushed. Cuts get 30 ms crossfades.
- **Reference audio:** not used. It is another workshop's noise, and laying it over our floor would misrepresent where
  the sound comes from. Ours is our own, so this master is postable as is, including in ads.
- **Text:** "THE / SOUND OF / CRAFT" in Inter 600, 34 px, cream, tracked out, at the reference's three staggered
  places, inside the safe zone.
  - The word "handmade" is avoided here because the CNC router is on screen; the reel only claims "craft".
- **Grade:** the MOTION.md montage grade. No faces in any shot.
