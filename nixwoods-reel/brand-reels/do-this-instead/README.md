# NixWoods "Stop guessing. Do this instead.": a five-step how-to on nixwoods.com

**The idea:** @marcelodesignxh's reel (instagram.com/reel/DccR9uIh5OO, 15.4 s, 719k views, 7.7k comments) teaches
one thing: don't start from a blank page; start from a strong reference and follow numbered steps.

Its format, from the frames:
- a hook bubble over a strong first shot ("✋ Stop using AI to design from scratch. Use this instead.");
- numbered pills over floating browser windows on a dark grid ("1. Go to HorizonX", "2. Explore", "3. Pick your
  template" …);
- the result;
- a comment-keyword call to action. That CTA is what drives its comment count.

The owner asked on 16 Oct: "Can you make something with the knowledge this reel gives?"

**Ours:** the same format on our own site, ending on the lamp lit in a real room. 15.4 s.

| Time | Pill | Screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0 | (hook) | "✋ Stop guessing which lamp fits your corner. Do this instead." The 5 Aug shoot camera moves in on the Minimalist floor lamp. |
| 3.0 | 1. Go to nixwoods.com | The address typed, then the home page |
| 5.4 | 2. Pick your room | The cursor goes to "Wooden Floor Lamps" in the nav, then the collection |
| 7.4 | 3. Pick your lamp | A push in on the Minimalist floor lamp card |
| 9.6 | 4. Check the size | The size chart, then the 44 in / 60 in buttons |
| 11.6 | 5. Light up your corner | The lamp, lit by an arched window, grows out of the window to full frame |
| 13.2 | 👇 Comment LAMP for the link | |

**Screens:**
- Real captures of nixwoods.com (Chromium, 1280 px at 2x, 16 Oct), so every word and price on screen is the site's own.
- `capture.py` saves them to `work/`, which is not committed.
- Re-capture before posting if the site changes.

**Look:**
- MOTION.md Dusk ground with a faint cream grid, standing in for the reference's dark grid.
- Cream pills and bubbles in teak ink, Inter 600.
- Amber click rings.

**Sound:**
- Our own: music3-design, typed keys, clicks and a whoosh on each step.
- No in-app-audio master. The reference's audio is its creator talking about a design tool, which does not fit here.
- Postable as is, and can run as an ad (ads go to .com).
