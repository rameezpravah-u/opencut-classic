## Verdict for NixWoods: keep Fraunces (headlines) + Inter (price, facts)

- **All seven are logo or tech-brand faces.** Each slide is a single wordmark in a geometric sans, and
  that reads as a gadget. A rounded geometric headline turns "solid teak, handmade" into an app
  launch. Fraunces is the warm, slightly irregular serif that carries the wood.
- **The two free ones fail on price.** Outfit and Urbanist have no ₹ glyph, so ₹999 renders as a
  missing-character box. See `NW-font-test-endcard.jpg`: the same end card in Fraunces + Inter, Outfit
  and Urbanist. Inter has ₹ and is already the "clean" face, on prices and chips.
- **The five paid or unknown fonts** would each need a licence check and a ₹ check before use. None
  of them does a job Inter doesn't already do.
- **What to take from the post** is its format, not its fonts: a numbered list carousel with
  "Save it for later" on slide 1 and a swipe count. That suits a NixWoods list post, e.g.
  "5 corners that need a warm light".
