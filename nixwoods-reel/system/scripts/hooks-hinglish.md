# Hinglish hooks for the first two seconds

**Source:** the owner sent a cheat sheet on 14 Oct 2026, instagram.com/p/Dd1EOg2j5va by @aashish_ai_engineer.
- **What it is:** 20 "instant hooks" in Hinglish, fill-in-the-blank, to grab attention in the first two seconds.
- **How it did:** 3.1k likes and 1.1k comments.

The templates below are filled in for NixWoods. We keep the sentence shapes, which are the reusable part; the
lines are ours.

## Rules for using these
- **Every line must be true.** The facts come from `brand.md` and the live product pages:
  - solid wood, handmade in Uttar Pradesh;
  - a warm 2700–3000K glow;
  - plug in, no electrician, no assembly;
  - NixLine ₹999 with cash on delivery (COD is confirmed for the NixLine only).
- **Check prices before you post.** Re-check every ₹ on Shopify on the day.
- **"Owner only" lines tell a first-person story.** Use one only after Rameez confirms it happened. We never invent a
  maker's story or a customer's, just as we never invent a testimonial.
- **Copy rules:** no banned words (brand.md), and at most 12 words on screen.
- **On screen:** the hook goes on screen in Hinglish, with the product lit behind it, and is said aloud too if the reel
  has a voice. Keep it on screen for at least 1.5 s.
