## Rules used here

- Hook → Problem → Turn → Proof → CTA. The "turn" is always the mechanism (turn the block = colour changes).
- Sentences under 12 words. Contractions. No announcer voice.
- The CTA repeats the benefit in operational language ("turn the block"), then the URL.
- Banned: discover, elevate, luxury, premium, exquisite, journey, curated, unlock, stunning, delve, realm.
- Never put a testimonial in VO unless it is a real quote with a name.
