Step by step

  1. 1Mark the position. Hold the canopy flat to the ceiling and mark its screw holes. Check the two wire drop points line up with the mounting points on the light.
  2. 2Drill and plug. Drill to suit the wall plugs provided — match the hole depth to the plug length. Push each plug in until flush.
  3. 3Fix the canopy. Screw it tight to the ceiling and check it sits level. It carries the full weight of the light — do not leave it hand-tight.
  4. 4Attach the suspension wire. This is the one you fit, at both ends: lock it into the canopy, then into the light. Tug down firmly on each end to confirm it has locked.
  5. 5Seat the 2-in-1 wire. It already runs from your light. Seat its canopy end fully and tug down to confirm it has locked before you let go of any weight.
  6. 6Your electrician connects the supply. Live, Neutral and Earth join the 2-in-1 wire at the canopy. Do not attempt this yourself.
  7. 7Level it and set the height. Set both wires to exactly the same length or the light will hang crooked. Adjust at the gripper, then pull down gently to lock.
  8. 8Power on and use your remote. It’s already paired — nothing to set up.
  9. 9Final check. Canopy screws tight · both wires locked · light hangs level · no wobble.

The one thing that actually goes wrong

The two wires set to different lengths — the light hangs crooked. Measure both from canopy to light and match them exactly. It takes two minutes to avoid and a lot longer to undo.

Before you drill, and after you hang

Open only what you need.

What kind of ceiling do you have?
Concrete or solid: use the wall plugs provided.

POP or gypsum false ceiling: the canopy must be anchored to the support frame above the board, or fixed with gypsum-rated toggle anchors. Never plain plugs in plasterboard — they will pull out.

Two people make this much easier: one to hold, one to fix.
How high should it hang?
Over a dining table: 75–90 cm above the tabletop.

Open areas and walkways: minimum 210 cm floor clearance.

Sit down at the table before you lock the height — the frame should rest just above your eye line when seated.
It’s not working
Won’t turn on: check the MCB and the wall switch, then have your electrician re-check the connection to the 2-in-1 wire at the canopy.

Hangs crooked: almost always the two wires are different lengths. Re-measure both from canopy to light and match them exactly.

A wire slips: the gripper locks on a downward pull. Take the weight off, re-seat the wire fully, then tug down firmly.

Remote won’t work: it’s paired at the workshop, so it should just work. Check the battery first, then message us.

Looking after solid wood
Dust with a soft, dry cloth. No chemical or wet cleaners.

Solid wood breathes. In humid months, slight movement in the grain is natural — it is proof your piece is real hardwood, not laminate.

Keep it out of prolonged direct sunlight. Once or twice a year, a drop of natural wood oil on a cloth keeps the finish rich.
