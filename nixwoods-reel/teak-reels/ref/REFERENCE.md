# Design reference — Double Arm Teak Pendant

**The product**: a single 49-inch bar of solid teak with **two** parallel LED channels cut into the underside, suspended on two black cables from a teak canopy. 49 × 2 × 3 in. 3000K, 18W, dimmable by remote. ₹9,999. The twin lines are the whole design — a cut that shows only one strip is wrong, and there is no shade or exposed bulb.

**Reference imagery**: `../hf/sh*` are the live PDP photographs from the Shopify CDN, 6 Sep 2026 — real photography. `sh03-dark-two-lines.jpg` is the hero: a dark room with both strips reading as two clean lines, and it is the best hook frame in the set. `sh01-underside-twin.jpg` and `sh04-end-channels.webp` prove the twin channels close up.

**Framing**: horizontal fixture; wide and underside shots use `fit: contain`, the dark-room hero is cropped with `crop_cy` biased slightly low so both lines sit in the upper third with room for copy beneath.
