# Design reference — Scandinavian Rosewood Pendant

**The product**: one cylindrical bar of solid rosewood (or teakwood) suspended on two thin black cables from a matching wood canopy, with a warm LED strip running along the underside. 4 ft (48 in) or 6 ft (72 in). 3000K, 18W, dimmable by remote, cable height adjustable. From ₹7,599. It has no shade and no visible bulb; the wood *is* the fixture, and the ends are softly rounded.

**Reference imagery**: `../hf/sh*` are the live PDP photographs from the Shopify CDN, 6 Sep 2026 — real photography. `sh01-endcap-macro.jpg` shows the rounded end and grain, `sh06-led-underside.jpg` the strip, `sh02-evening-six.jpg` the lit dining scene.
