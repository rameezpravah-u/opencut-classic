# NixWoods product facts (what each product page actually says)

Built 16 Oct 2026 from live Shopify. Re-check prices on the day of posting.

Source: Shopify Admin, `status:active` (38 products), fields handle, title, productType, tags, options,
variants (price, compare-at), description and featured image. Every fact below is from that product's own page
text. "not stated" means the page does not say it, so a reel must not claim it either. Read `PRODUCT-ID.md` first
to identify the product. This file says what you may claim about it once you know which one it is.

Rules that apply to every product:
- **Rosewood and Sheesham are used as one wood on these pages** ("solid rosewood (Sheesham)", "Sheesham (Indian
  rosewood)"). If a page says only one of them, use that one word.
- **Warranty**, where a page states it: 3 years on the wood, joinery and craft, plus 1 year on LED and electricals.
- **Trust block** on most pages: free replacement if it arrives damaged (message within 48 h with order ID and a
  photo), ₹100 off prepaid with code FLY100, WhatsApp support +91 92860 01060, handcrafted in Uttar Pradesh (some
  pages say Khatauli).
- **Delivery**, where a page states it: dispatched in about 3 working days, delivered in roughly 5 to 7, free
  shipping across India **on prepaid orders**. Do not say "free delivery" without "prepaid".
- **COD.** Six pages say: "We do not take full cash on delivery. Pay 10% online to confirm your order and the balance
  on delivery." No product page offers full COD. See the NixLine floor lamp section and Discrepancies D1.
- **Dimmable** is claimed only where the page says so. **Remote** is claimed only where the page says so.

## Discrepancies (title vs tags vs handle vs page vs PRODUCT-ID)

- **D1 · COD and free delivery.** PRODUCT-ID line 14 says "COD and free delivery are confirmed for the NixLine floor lamp only". The NixLine floor lamp page says nothing about COD or delivery. Six other pages say "We do not take full cash on delivery; pay 10% online", and free shipping applies to **prepaid** orders only (Aure, Aurora, Timber, Rubik's Cube, Scandinavian round, 30" sconce). Get the owner to confirm the NixLine COD offer before using it.
